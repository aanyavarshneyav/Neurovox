8. Scope, Provenance & Limitations

Scaled Reproduction on LibriSpeech (251 Speakers)
Trained on LibriSpeech train-clean-100 (251 speakers, 28,539 utterances measured) with scaled widths (C=32, C1=256, d_attn=128 → 4,167,700 params measured) rather than VoxCeleb2 (5,994 speakers, C=128, C1=512 → 30.91M params paper) to prevent small-cohort overfitting and fit local Apple M3 Pro (18 GB unified memory) budgets. Test sets contain 29 qualifying multi-chapter speakers each, yielding ~±1.0% 95% speaker-bootstrap CI widths.
Engineering Choices vs. Paper Facts
  • Paper Facts paper: 80-d log-Mel (25ms/10ms), 180-frame segments, B=64 distinct speakers, 2D-CNN → TDNN-LSTM → Fully-TDNN → MLFC → MLASP → 2×FC (192-d FC1 primary embedding), s=30, m=0.2, τ=0.423, ESD=5.
  • Engineering Choices: Scaled widths (C=32, C1=256, d_attn=128), ESC=5, min_keep_ratio=0.5 collapse guard (ADR-001), fp32 local-stats variance clamp (≥1e-5), replicate padding, on-the-fly babble + colored noise (replacing MUSAN), RIR disabled on Mac.
  • External-Unverified: τ_corr=0.5, SKA branch dilations (d=1, 2) & reduction ratio (r=8), local stats window W=21.
Open Discrepancy: S1 → S5 Parameter Jump (OPEN_QUESTIONS Q2)
Alam et al. Table II reports S1 = 30.91M paper and S5 (Vanilla 2D-CNN) = 17.37M paper — a reduction of 13.54M (-43.8%).

However, in our torchinfo-audited implementation preserving all Table VIII channel widths, replacing fwSKA + cwSKA with standard 3×3 Conv2d inside the two ResBlocks reduces parameters from 4,167,700 (S1 measured) to 4,109,728 (S5 measured) — a delta of only 57,972 (-1.39%) at scaled width (or ~1.49M at full width C=128). Even removing the entire 2D-CNN frontend at full scale only removes 1.65M in the conv blocks. Thus, the paper's 13.54M drop between S1 and S5 cannot come solely from swapping SKA for vanilla Conv2d at fixed channel widths and remains an open published discrepancy.
